Is there a relationship between hypertension and stroke? $$$ **Short answer:**\nYes.  Hypertension is a well‑established, dose‑dependent risk factor for all types of stroke, and lowering blood pressure consistently reduces that risk.\n---\n## 1.  Introduction\nStroke is a leading cause of death and disability worldwide.  Epidemiologic studies and randomized trials have repeatedly shown that elevated systolic and diastolic blood pressure (BP) increases the likelihood of both ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke.  The relationship is graded: each 10 mm Hg rise in systolic BP (or 5 mm Hg in diastolic BP) is associated with a roughly 30–35 % higher stroke risk.  Conversely, each 10 mm Hg systolic reduction (or 5 mm Hg diastolic) lowers stroke risk by a similar magnitude.\n## 2.
